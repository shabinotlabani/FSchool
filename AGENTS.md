# Project preferences

- Display monetary amounts as numbers without a currency symbol. Do not add the euro sign (Unicode U+20AC) to screens, forms, reports, printouts, validation messages, or generated descriptions. Preserve this preference in future changes.
- Use tabular grids with rows and columns for lists, reports, summaries, and selections instead of card layouts. Keep tables on mobile with horizontal scrolling where needed. Preserve this preference in future changes.
- Use a consistent, balanced light interface throughout the platform, with flat sections and tabular grids rather than cards, including remaining sponsorship budgets.
